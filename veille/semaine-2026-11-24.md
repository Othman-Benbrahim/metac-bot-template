Family 30 makes that question a research program rather than a promise. The difficult distinction is between a useful output and a useful computational mechanism. A hybrid may succeed because classical preprocessing finds the hard structure, because a warm start contains most of the answer or because a repair routine makes an infeasible sample usable. A quantum classifier may seem accurate because neighboring windows leak session identity. A speedup may disappear when encoding, queueing, readout and unsuccessful attempts are counted. The research therefore compares each candidate against an independently qualified classical portfolio and an ablation of the claimed quantum contribution.  The cultural inheritance is deliberately interpreted as a philosophy of complementary constraints: initiative and restraint, movement and stillness, hypothesis and its counterexample. Yin and yang do not become SI observables by analogy. Bioelectric signals supply dimensional input to a phantom model, not evidence of a quantum therapeutic field. A simulation result can motivate a future experiment, but cannot certify efficacy, a physical new law or authority over a device.
